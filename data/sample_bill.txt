PART I - PRELIMINARY

Section 1 - Short Title
This Bill may be cited as the Plateau State Basic Education (Amendment) Bill, 2026.

Section 2 - Commencement
This Bill shall come into force on the date of its signing into law by the Governor.

Section 3 - Interpretation
In this Bill, unless the context otherwise requires:
"Basic Education" means education provided to children from primary one through junior secondary three.
"Board" means the Plateau State Universal Basic Education Board.
"Ministry" means the Ministry of Education of Plateau State.

PART II - ADMINISTRATION

Section 4 - Establishment of the Board
(1) There is established for the State a body to be known as the Plateau State Universal Basic Education Board.
(2) The Board shall be responsible for the implementation of basic education policy across the State.

Section 5 - Functions of the Board
(1) The Board shall:
    (a) provide free, compulsory basic education to every child of school age;
    (b) develop infrastructure for basic education institutions;
    (c) recruit, train, and deploy qualified teaching staff;
    (d) monitor compliance with basic education standards across Local Government Areas.

Section 6 - Funding
(1) The Board shall be funded through:
    (a) annual budgetary allocation from the State Government;
    (b) contributions from the Universal Basic Education Commission;
    (c) grants, donations, and other sources approved by the Ministry.

PART III - COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT

Section 7 - Compulsory Enrolment
(1) Every parent or guardian resident in the State shall enrol every child under their care in a basic education institution.
(2) Failure to comply with subsection (1) without reasonable cause is an offence.

Section 8 - Penalties
(1) A person who contravenes Section 7 is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding fifty thousand naira or community service as determined by a Magistrate Court.

PART IV - MISCELLANEOUS

Section 9 - Regulations
The Ministry may make regulations for the effective implementation of this Bill.

Section 10 - Repeal
The Plateau State Basic Education Law, 2010 is hereby repealed.
